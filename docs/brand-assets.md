# Brand asset sources

Technology icons use official brand assets rather than hand-drawn approximations. The Node.js project icon is the original hexagon logo published by the Node.js project:

- [Node.js brand policy and logo gallery](https://nodejs.org/ja/about/branding)
- [Official Node.js hexagon SVG](https://github.com/nodejs/nodejs.org/blob/main/apps/site/public/static/logos/nodejsHex.svg)

The existing language icons are sourced from established brand-mark artwork. Keep Poligo's own logo and favicon separate from third-party brand marks. When adding or replacing a technology icon, use the relevant upstream artwork, retain its brand proportions, and include an accessible title where appropriate.
